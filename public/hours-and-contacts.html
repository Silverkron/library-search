Opening Hours

Opening hours may change during national and local holidays. Contact the library by telephone to confirm access arrangements.

Contact Information

Borrowing

Frequently Asked Questions

What time does the Aci Sant'Antonio library open?
The library is open to the public Monday to Friday, from 09:00 to 12:30. On Thursday, there is also afternoon opening from 14:30 to 18:00.
Is the library open on Saturday or Sunday?
Currently, the Aci Sant'Antonio Library is closed on Saturday and Sunday. Hours may vary during holidays. Refer to the institutional website or contact the library for updates.
How can I contact the municipal library of Aci Sant'Antonio?
Contact the library at phone number 095 7010094 or by email at comune-acisantantonio@legalmail.it
Who can I contact for information about events or cultural initiatives?
For information about events, meetings, or cultural activities organized by the library, contact library staff directly at 095 7010094 or write an email to the official address. Alternatively, follow updates on the Municipality of Aci Sant'Antonio website.
How do I know if the library is open today?
To confirm opening hours for a specific day:
  • Check the updated hours on the official website
  • Contact 095 7010094 during office hours
  • Check the library's Google Maps page, if active
