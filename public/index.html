Frequently Asked Questions

How many books does the Aci Sant'Antonio Library have?
This version of the online catalogue contains 9289 bibliographic records. This count describes the data published on this website, not loan availability or the library’s entire collection.
Where can I consult the library's online catalog?
The online catalog can be consulted directly from the homepage, through the internal search engine. You can search for a book by title, author, publication year, or inventory number.
Can I borrow books from the library?
Contact library staff to confirm registration requirements, book availability and return dates before using the lending service.
What services does the municipal library of Aci Sant'Antonio offer?
The library provides consultation, lending and bibliographic assistance. For cultural activities, refer to municipal announcements or contact the library.

Catalogue data

The catalogue contains bibliographic records, not real-time loan availability. Inventory numbers are not ISBNs.
