### Description

The Municipal Library is located in the former town hall on Via Vittorio Emanuele, in the historic centre of Aci Sant’Antonio.

For information about access to the premises, contact library staff.

### How to Get Here

From Catania: Take the SS121 heading north towards Acireale, follow the signs for Aci Sant'Antonio and continue to Via Vittorio Emanuele.

By public transport: You can reach Aci Sant'Antonio using AST or FCE bus lines from Catania. The nearest stop is just a few minutes' walk from the library.

Parking: Several parking areas are available near the library for those arriving by car.

## Local landmarks

### Main Church

A short distance from the library is the Main Church of Aci Sant'Antonio, an important religious and cultural building in the town.

### Town Hall

The new Town Hall is located nearby, with the main municipal offices and services for citizens.

### Main Square

Piazza Maggiore is a landmark in the historic centre.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is the Municipal Library of Aci Sant'Antonio located?

The library is located at Via Vittorio Emanuele 101 (or 105), in the historic center of Aci Sant'Antonio (CT), inside the former town hall building. It is easily reachable on foot from Piazza Maggiore.

### How can I get to the Aci Sant'Antonio library?

Access options:

- By car, following the SS114 or SP4, with parking possibilities nearby

- By bus, with urban lines that stop near the center

- On foot, if you are already in the center of Aci Sant'Antonio (Piazza Maggiore area)

### Is there parking near the library?

Yes, free and paid parking is available around the library along Via Vittorio Emanuele and nearby streets. It is recommended to arrive a few minutes early during events or peak hours.

### What places of interest are there near the library?

In the immediate vicinity you will find:

- Piazza Maggiore, heart of the city center

- The Mother Church of San Antonio Abate

### Is the library accessible to people with disabilities?

Contact library staff before visiting for information about entrances, accessible routes and any assistance required.
